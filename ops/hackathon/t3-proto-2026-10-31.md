## 1. Executive Summary

Task 3 delivers a complete end-to-end rewrite of the DayPlay AR Corridor Simulator (`sim/app.js` and `sim/index.html`) connecting typed intents to interactive first-person field-of-view (FOV) placard rendering along the Mission District corridor.

All rendered placards are strictly verified via the backend zero-hallucination gate (`POST /pipeline`). In Stage B, two clean 1920x1080 30fps H.264 walkthrough videos were captured, latency metrics were extracted across multiple intent passes, and QA visual verification confirmed correct placard legibility, FOV entry/exit, countdown meters, and kind badges.

---

## 2. Changes Delivered

1. **`sim/app.js` (Pipeline Integration & FOV Engine):**
   - Direct HTTP integration with `POST /pipeline` on same origin.
   - Grounded geometry engine: `metersPerDeg` computation (`lat = 111320`, `lng = 111320 * cos(lat)`).
   - Dynamic walkthrough: user position advances along Valencia/Mission corridor (heading ~35° NW) with live distance countdowns and dynamic FOV clipping (62° HFOV).
   - Kind badges & styling: distinct icons and badges for `saved`, `event`, and `plan_stop` listings.
   - High-contrast 1920x1080 legible placards with distance meters, start times, and entity IDs.
   - Latency logging: `window.__latencyLog` logs intent submission, pipeline response latency, and DOM render latency.
   - Speed controls: toggle walking state (ON/PAUSED), speed multiplier (1x, 3x, 6x), and corridor reset.

2. **`sim/index.html` (AR HUD):**
   - Clean, dark transparent HUD displaying heading, live coordinates, speed, and active pin count.
   - Viewfinder reticle and compass indicator.
   - Controls bar for walk manipulation during demos.
   - Intent input box (`#q`) with instant submission to `/pipeline`.

## 5. QA Verdict

- **Aspect Ratio & Resolution:** 1920x1080 at 30fps confirmed via `ffprobe` (`width=1920`, `height=1080`, `pix_fmt=yuv420p`, `r_frame_rate=30/1`). Zero letterboxing, zero black bars.
- **Placard Legibility:** Verified at 1920x1080. High-contrast typography on `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.94)` background with cyan borders (`#7dd3fc`).
- **FOV Geometry & Frustum:** Pins enter FOV when bearing is within ±34.1° (62° HFOV * 0.55 margin) and distance is within 1m–600m; pins exit properly behind the user or outside the frustum.
- **Distance Countdowns:** Real-time distance update in meters verified in both slow and fast traversals.
- **Kind Badges & Icons:** Verified rendering correct badges (`SAVED`, `EVENT`, `STOP`) and icons (`★`, `⚡`, `📍`).
- **Zero Hallucination:** 100% of rendered pins map to verified `corridor_real.json` listing IDs with passing assertion gate.
- **Verdict:** **PASS (Production Ready)**.
